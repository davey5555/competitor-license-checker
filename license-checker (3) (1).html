SEGOB / DGJS (MX) sijscasinos.segob.gob.mx — search by legal entity name, not domain
Curaçao (CGA) ⚠️ No domain search tool exists — cert.cga.cw only shows individual certificates via unique links generated when you click the CGA seal directly ON the operator's own site (usually a green "CGA" box in the footer). If the seal is missing, doesn't link to cert.cga.cw, or the certificate shows a different domain than the one you're checking, treat it as unauthorized. Reformed 24 Dec 2024; pre-reform "master license" numbers (incl. #365/JAZ, #1668/JAZ, #8048/JAZ, #5536/JAZ, and any Antillephone / Cyberluck / Gaming Services Provider N.V. reference) are now defunct
Malta (MGA) ✅ Confirmed-fraud blacklist (297 entries, 2006–present) checked automatically above. For genuine licensee verification: mgaurlchecker.mga.org.mt (dedicated URL checker) or mga.org.mt/licensee-hub/licensee-register (name/licence-number search)
Anjouan ✅ Confirmed working domain-search tool at anjouangaming.com/public-register/verify-a-licence (button above). Caution: several other sites also claim to be "official" (anjoauangaming.org, anjouongamingboard.org, gamingcontrolanjouan.org) — this fragmentation is itself a red flag, and even a genuine match here means lighter oversight than the other regulators
